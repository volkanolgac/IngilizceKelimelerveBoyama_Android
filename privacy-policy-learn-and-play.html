Privacy Policy

Learn & Play - Kids English & Turkish Word Game
Effective date: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Learn & Play - Kids English & Turkish Word Game ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

In short: The App is designed to work without requiring an account and does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information with us. The App does not contain advertising or user-tracking features.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information through the App. The App does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or other identifying information in order to use its core features.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store limited preferences locally on your device, such as the user's selected pronunciation voice. This information is stored locally and is not transmitted to us.

Game progress, preferences, and similar information, where applicable, are intended to remain on the user's device and are not associated with an online user account.

3. Speech and Audio Features

The App includes English pronunciation and audio features. Depending on the device and Android system configuration, speech synthesis may be provided by the device's built-in text-to-speech services. The App does not require the user to create an account for these features.

The App does not request microphone, camera, contacts, location, or other sensitive device permissions for its core functionality.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for children and families and is designed with children's privacy in mind. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

The App does not require children to provide personal information to access its core learning, word-game, coloring, puzzle, pronunciation, or sticker-book features.

5. Advertising and Tracking

The App does not intentionally include third-party advertising, behavioral advertising, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking functionality.

We do not sell personal information and do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on standard Android system functionality, such as the device's built-in text-to-speech engine, to provide pronunciation. Such system services are governed by the privacy policies of their respective providers and by the user's device settings.

Apart from such operating-system functionality, the App is designed so that its core features do not require sending personal information to a third-party server.

7. Data Security

Because the App does not require an online account and does not intentionally collect personal information, there is no personal-information database operated by us for this App. Locally stored preferences are subject to the security of the user's device and operating system.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a server-side account or personal-information record for App users. Locally stored App data can generally be removed by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage/data through Android device settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality or applicable legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published at the same privacy-policy location and will include a revised effective date.
